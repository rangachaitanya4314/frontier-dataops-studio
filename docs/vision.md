# Vision

## What is Frontier DataOps Studio?

Frontier DataOps Studio is an open-source platform that helps AI/ML teams manage the full lifecycle of their training data — from dataset ingestion and organization, through annotation and quality review, to analytics and export.

## Why?

Building high-quality AI models depends on high-quality data. Yet most teams cobble together spreadsheets, shared drives, and one-off scripts to manage their datasets. This creates bottlenecks, quality issues, and poor traceability.

Frontier DataOps Studio provides a unified workspace where teams can:

- **Organize** datasets into projects with clear metadata and versioning
- **Annotate** data with built-in tools for bounding boxes, classification, segmentation, and NER
- **Review** annotations with configurable quality checks and approval workflows
- **Analyze** annotation progress, inter-annotator agreement, and dataset statistics
- **Export** production-ready datasets in standard formats

## Guiding Principles

1. **Open source first** — The core platform is free and self-hostable
2. **Data quality over quantity** — Every feature should make it easier to produce better data, not just more data
3. **Developer experience** — Clean APIs, typed interfaces, and clear documentation
4. **Incremental adoption** — Teams should be able to start with dataset management and grow into annotation and review workflows over time

## Target Users

- ML engineers managing training datasets
- Annotation teams working on labeling tasks
- Data quality analysts reviewing annotation output
- Team leads tracking annotation project progress
